# Algebraic proof

The two polynomials are

Q(q)=9q⁴−14q³−30q²−14q+1,

H(q)=13q⁶−30q⁵−78q⁴−94q³−78q²−30q+1.

At q=1 they equal −48 and −296, respectively. For every integer q≥2, both are congruent to 1 modulo q, so neither can equal zero. This proves nonvanishing at every positive integer.

A separate conditional lemma assumes ΔX−ΔY=8(t−s)P with P>0. If both deficits are zero, the product is zero and P≠0 forces s=t. The factorization is a hypothesis, not a derived graph identity.
